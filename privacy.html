Privacy

Privacy notice.

How Current Security Group Ltd handles personal data submitted through this website, WhatsApp and email, under the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019.

Who we are

Current Security Group Ltd is a private security company with offices in Nairobi and Sirisia, Bungoma. For this notice, we are the data controller for personal data submitted through this website.

What we collect

  • Quote enquiries — name, phone number, email, service requested, location and the details you write in the message.
  • Client support requests — site name, contact name, phone number, incident details and priority.
  • Job applications — name, phone number, location, experience, availability and any notes you provide. CVs and ID copies are requested separately.
  • Training enquiries — name, organisation, contact details, course, participant numbers and preferred dates.
  • Technical data — this site does not set cookies and does not run third-party analytics or advertising scripts.

Why we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and prepare a quotation.
  • To arrange a site survey, training session or client support visit.
  • To consider a job application and complete vetting where you are shortlisted.
  • To provide and administer a security contract.
  • To meet legal, regulatory and insurance obligations.

Lawful basis

We rely on your consent when you submit a form, on steps taken at your request before entering a contract, on the performance of a contract, and on our legitimate interests in running and protecting the business. Where the law requires us to keep records, we rely on a legal obligation.

Who we share it with

We do not sell personal data. We may share it with:

  • WhatsApp / Meta — when you choose to send a form through WhatsApp. That message is handled under WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy.
  • Email providers — when you send or receive email from the addresses published on this site.
  • Regulators, insurers, auditors and law enforcement — where required by law, a contract or a formal investigation.
  • Service providers — only where they help us operate the business and are bound to protect the data.

How long we keep it

Enquiry records are kept for [TO CONFIRM] after the enquiry closes. Job applications are kept for [TO CONFIRM]. Client and contract records are kept for the period required by the contract, our insurers and the law. When data is no longer needed, it is deleted or securely disposed of.

Your rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019 you can ask for access to your personal data, correction of inaccurate data, deletion, restriction of processing, or object to processing. You can also withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing that already took place.

To exercise a right, contact us using the details below. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner at odpc.go.ke.

Security

Access to enquiry and application data is limited to staff who need it. Please do not send passwords, bank details, card numbers or unnecessary sensitive information through the website forms. For an emergency, call the hotline instead of using a form.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when our processes or the law change. The date below shows the last revision.
