Training

Practical skills for the day something goes wrong.

Corporate wellbeing and safety training for teams and individuals. The courses are built around prediction, avoidance and response, not theory for its own sake.

Request training Call 0737 334 449

01 — The approach

Your people are the first response.

Before a guard, an alarm or a camera can help, someone has to notice the risk and make a decision. Training gives your team a shared language for that moment.

  • Built around your risks — the course content is matched to your premises and routines.
  • Practical delivery — scenarios, discussion and drills rather than slides alone.
  • For teams or individuals — in-house sessions at your premises or scheduled courses.
The first responder is the person who notices.Training principle

What a session looks like

Practical, audience-appropriate content delivered at your premises or at a scheduled venue. Tell us the risks your team faces and we will shape the session around them.

02 — Course catalogue

Four areas of training.

Courses can be combined into a programme. Dates and fees are confirmed after we understand the audience and location.

Awareness

Recognising risk before it becomes an incident.

  • Security awareness & crime avoidance
  • Travel security
  • ATM crime avoidance

Personal safety

Skills for people who may be targeted outside the workplace.

  • Self defence
  • Women's self defence & rape prevention
  • Hostage and kidnap survival

Specialised

High-risk scenarios for teams that face them directly.

  • Armed robbery management
  • Bomb and IED awareness
  • Firearm skills

Driving

Vehicle control when the road or the situation turns.

  • Defensive driving
  • Skidpan driving

Specialised courses may have eligibility or legal requirements. We confirm these before accepting a booking.
