02 — Premises we secure

Different sites need different post orders.

A construction yard, a school and a residential estate do not carry the same risk. The guard on your gate should know the difference.

Residential estates

Access control, visitor management, night patrols and incident reporting for apartments and gated communities.

Commercial & retail

Loss prevention, customer-facing guards, cash-in-transit coordination and opening and closing procedures.

Construction & industrial

Plant and material protection, delivery control, perimeter checks and after-hours coverage.

Education

Gate management, visitor screening, pupil safety and liaison with school administration.

Healthcare

Controlled entry, crowd management, parking control and support for clinical teams.

Events & institutions

Crowd control, bag checks, accreditation checks and coordination with event organisers.

06 — Taking over

Five steps before an officer reaches your gate.

A resident contract manager oversees the transition and remains your point of contact after deployment.

  1. Site survey

    We assess access points, assets, hours and risks, then give written recommendations.

  2. Assignment instructions

    Post orders are agreed with you before the first shift.

  3. Property inventory

    Equipment, keys and property are recorded at the site.

  4. Guard introduction

    Officers are introduced before they are posted, with details of every guard assigned.

  5. Handover

    The contract manager manages the changeover and ongoing supervision.

07 — Verification

Check the badge. Then check with us.

Every officer carries a PSRA card and a Current Security staff ID. If someone at your gate cannot produce both, call the control room before you let them in.

  • PSRA card — photo, registration number and expiry date.
  • Company ID — officer name, staff number and posting.
  • Control room check — we confirm the posting with your site supervisor.
