Security guarding

Trained, vetted officers on 12-hour shifts for surveillance, deterrence and protection of your property. Posts are specified around your access points and routines rather than a generic guard package.

  • Vetted to Private Security Regulatory Authority criteria
  • One month of training plus practical induction beside experienced guards
  • Supervisors check each shift three times
  • Written assignment instructions agreed with you
  • Radios or mobile phones on site and with supervisors

02

K9 dog service

Trained security dogs and handlers for an added layer of deterrence, day and night. K9 teams are deployed alongside static guards where the site layout and risk profile call for a mobile presence.

  • Trained dogs and handlers
  • Deterrence day and night
  • Handler-led patrols of the site
  • Deployed as part of a guarding programme, not as a standalone post
Request K9 service
03

CCTV monitoring

Camera installation and training, with monitors manned around the clock for banks, industries, stores and homes. The system is only useful if someone is watching and knows what to do with what they see.

  • Cameras installed and your staff trained
  • Monitors manned around the clock
  • Coverage planned around access points, tills, stores and yards
  • Banks, industries, stores and homes
Ask about CCTV

Alarm systems

Automatic intrusion alarms and remote or push-button panic systems, installed and serviced. Alarms are configured to suit the site, with the response route agreed before installation.

  • Automatic intrusion alarms
  • Remote or push-button panic systems
  • Installed and serviced
  • Response route agreed with your guard post or control room
