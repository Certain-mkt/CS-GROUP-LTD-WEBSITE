Careers

Work with a firm that supervises its people.

We accept applications on an ongoing basis for guard and supervisory roles in Nairobi and Bungoma. If you are willing to be vetted, trained and held to a standard, start here.

Apply now How recruitment works

01 — What we look for

Judgement, not just presence.

A guard is trusted with property, keys, access and sometimes people. We look for people who can follow instructions, write a clear report and stay calm when the routine breaks.

  • Vetting — you must meet the Private Security Regulatory Authority criteria.
  • Literacy — reading, writing and clear incident reporting.
  • Fitness — a physical and medical examination before deployment.
  • Training — one month of training plus practical induction alongside experienced guards.
  • Reliability — shifts are 12 hours and posts must be covered.

We never charge a recruitment fee.

Current Security Group Ltd does not ask applicants for money at any stage: not for application, vetting, training, uniform or deployment. If someone claiming to represent us asks for payment, stop and call 0737 334 449.

What to prepare

A current CV, a copy of your ID, your phone number and any previous security or supervisory experience. You will be asked to provide original documents during vetting.

02 — Recruitment process

Four stages. No shortcuts.

Every officer goes through the same process before they are posted. Training is not optional, and vetting is completed before deployment.

  1. Application

    Send your details through the form or WhatsApp. Include your CV and ID copy.

  2. Vetting

    PSRA checks, documents, references and a medical and physical examination.

  3. Training

    One month of training, then practical induction beside experienced guards.

  4. Deployment

    Assignment to a site, with details recorded and supervision in place.

03 — Application

Start an application.

Complete the form and it will open WhatsApp with your details ready to send. Attach your CV and ID copy in the same chat.

  • Be accurate. Vetting will confirm your documents and references.
  • Use a phone number you answer. We call shortlisted applicants.
  • Do not pay anyone. Our recruitment process is free.

04 — Questions

Applicant FAQ

Short answers for people considering an application.

Do I need security experience?

Not necessarily. We train new officers. Experience helps, but reliability, literacy and the ability to follow instructions matter more.

Is there a fee for training or uniform?

No. Current Security Group Ltd does not charge applicants for application, vetting, training, uniform or deployment. If anyone asks you for payment, call the hotline.

Where are the jobs based?

Our offices are in Nairobi and Sirisia, Bungoma. Deployment depends on client sites and the needs of the operation.

How long does recruitment take?

It depends on vetting and training schedules. We call shortlisted applicants with the next step.

Ready to be vetted, trained and posted?

Send your details. If you are shortlisted, we will call you.
