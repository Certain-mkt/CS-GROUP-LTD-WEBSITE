About the company

Current Security Group Ltd provides professional security services to homes, businesses and institutions across Kenya. The company has operated since 2004 and runs a 24/7 control room with a radio network linking guards, supervisors and duty managers.

Our approach is operational: every site is surveyed, the post instructions are written down and agreed, and supervisors check each guard three times per shift. A resident contract manager oversees the transition from your previous provider and remains the point of contact for the account.

Services

Security guarding

Trained, vetted officers on 12-hour shifts for surveillance, deterrence and protection of property. Guards are vetted to Private Security Regulatory Authority criteria, complete one month of training and are inducted beside experienced officers before taking a post alone.

K9 dog service

Trained dogs and handlers for an added layer of deterrence, day and night, deployed alongside static guards where the site layout and risk profile require a mobile presence.

CCTV and alarms

Camera installation and staff training, with monitors manned around the clock. Automatic intrusion alarms and remote or push-button panic systems are installed and serviced.

Access control and investigations

Keyless entry, fingerprint readers, airlock doors and remote door locks for single doors or multi-site operations. Investigators handle robbery, forgery, infiltration and mismanagement, with written reports and exhibits.

Consultancy and crowd control

Site surveys, vulnerability assessments and custom security programmes, plus recruitment and training of guards. Crowd control is provided for seminars, public gatherings, workshops and functions.

How we work

  • Vetting: PSRA criteria, literacy, and a physical and medical examination.
  • Training: one month covering access control, firefighting drills, first aid, crime detection and prevention, and customer care.
  • Supervision: three checks per guard per shift, plus unannounced duty-manager spot checks.
  • Transition: site survey, written recommendations, assignment instructions, property inventory and guard introductions.
  • Cover: fire, theft, burglary, WIBA and contractual insurance.
