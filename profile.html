About the company

Current Security (CS) was established in 2004 as a business entity under the name Wakobe Security Company, incorporated in 2013 as Wakobe Security Company Ltd, and in 2021 changed its name to Current Security Group Ltd, with a view to providing quality, professional security services to the public and private sectors. We are a Kenyan-based security provider with a niche in skills training and capacity building around security solutions.

We have a recognised track record of delivering specialised security solutions such as anti-terror training, close protection, risk and disaster management, investigations and related training and education, for the corporate sector, government agencies, foundations and related organisations, high net worth individuals and private individuals. With offices in Nairobi and Bungoma and an extensive network of associates, we bring our clients proven security solutions.

Our driving force is people with the dedication, dynamism and determination to be the best. Our motto, providing security services with diversity, has been our strength for growth, and it has helped us secure and maintain security contracts with reputable establishments. Whether your business is large or small, we provide a full range of security services built to meet your expectations.

Security guarding

We provide security guards on 12-hour shifts across a wide range of assignments. The main objective is surveillance, deterrence and the general protection and care of our customers’ property.

Every guard is selected and vetted to Private Security Regulatory Authority criteria, completes one month of training and works alongside experienced guards for practical induction before taking a post alone.

Supervisors visit each post three times per shift, and duty managers make unannounced spot checks, so replacements and emergency postings leave no gaps.

Technology, investigation and advisory services

01 CCTV monitoring

We install CCTV cameras and train staff at strategic points in assignments such as banks, industries, stores and residential houses. The monitor is positioned strategically and manned 24 hours a day while recording all activity.

02 Alarm systems

Automatic alarms activate as soon as they detect intrusion or interference. Remote-controlled and push-button alarms are activated manually by clients’ guards to call for help when under attack or sensing danger.

03 Access control

Multiple product lines of full access control systems, from keyless entry for a single door to a multi-site operation, including fingerprint detectors, airlock doors and remote-controlled door locks.

04 Private investigations

Highly trained investigators for robberies, forgeries, infiltration and any kind of mismanagement or crime-related issue. Clients are furnished with concrete reports and exhibits for ease of prosecution.

05 Security consultancy

Security advice, site surveys, and recruitment and training of guards and officers for companies and individuals. Our experts evaluate your programme using surveys, vulnerability assessments, crime statistics and accepted standards to design a customised one.

06 Crowd control

Non-violent and violent crowd control at seminars, public gatherings, workshops, functions and buildings.

Corporate wellbeing and safety training

Current Security provides capacity-building skills that strengthen security in the public and private sectors. The aim of corporate security training is to introduce participants to the realities of violence and crime. We give them the mental tools and understanding to proactively identify, avoid and manage these situations should they occur.

After completing a course, participants have a heightened awareness of personal security, which reduces their exposure to violent encounters and crime in every part of their lives. The knowledge and skills they learn help them lead healthier, safer lives, which means higher productivity in the workplace. We have wide experience in the design, development and implementation of successful training programmes in the public and private sectors.

How we take over a site

We can ensure a smooth transition from the incumbent service provider within 12 hours of the contract starting. At installation we make sure of the following.

1 Site survey Carried out at the earliest time possible, with advice given to you.
2 Instructions Concise, clear assignment instructions agreed with you.
3 Guards introduced Personnel introduced before posting, and you receive the details of every guard on your property.
4 Inventory All property in the area of assignment is taken stock of.
5 Oversight A resident contract manager oversees the transition.

Smooth takeover within 12 hours of the contract starting.

Communication network

We keep a constant and open network on radio and mobile phones, supplementing landline links with our control room and managers. All of our services have a radio call communication network so operations stay efficient and effective.

Supervision

Guards are supervised on their shifts, day and night, at their respective assignments, three times by supervisors. Assignment in-charges and supervisors carry either radios (walkie-talkies) or mobile phones for easy communication.

Duty managers also make impromptu spot checks to make sure the quality of service is never compromised, posting guards in emergencies and replacements. They travel by company vehicle, so there are no gaps and customers’ welfare and property are secured at all times.
